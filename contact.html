Frequently asked questions

What's the minimum quantity of plastic waste you accept?

1 tonne per load for scheduled collection. Smaller quantities can be discussed for drop-off at the plant – mention your volume in the supply enquiry.

Which plastic types do you accept?

We accept PET, HDPE, LDPE and PP. See the full accepted and not-accepted list on the Sell Plastic page.

Can I get a sample of the products before ordering?

Yes – samples can be arranged case by case ahead of a larger order. Mention this in your purchase enquiry.

Are you compliant with pollution control regulations?

We operate under Karnataka State Pollution Control Board (KSPCB) guidelines and follow Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) norms.

What areas do you serve?

Primarily Karnataka. Other regions are considered case by case, depending on logistics. Tell us your location in the enquiry and we'll confirm.

General enquiry

Real people behind our contact channels, not chatbots. We typically respond within 24 hours.
