Quality guidelines for supplied material

These aren't hard rejections. They keep your load moving straight to processing instead of needing rework at our site – and that affects how quickly we can confirm pricing and pickup.

Contamination

Free of glass, metal, sand or soil where possible. Some residue and food contamination is fine – heavy mixing with non-plastics is not.

Moisture

Reasonably dry material processes faster. Wet baled loads are still accepted, but may affect pricing.

Sorting

For the best possible price, sort plastic by type. Low mixing, such as HDPE with LDPE, is accepted. Pricing may vary.

Packaging

Loose, baled or bagged – whatever is practical for your operation and transport.

Logistics

  • For loads of 1 tonne or more, we can arrange a scheduled pickup within Vijayapura and neighbouring districts. Transport cost is factored into the price.
  • For smaller quantities or one-off sales, you can drop off material at our plant.
  • Waste pickers can register with us at the plant or on WhatsApp, and then drop off material directly.
