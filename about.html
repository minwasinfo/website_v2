About MINWAS

What?

MINWAS turns plastic waste into consistent, traceable recycled granules, keeping valuable material in use instead of in landfills.

Our plant follows CPCB and KSPCB environmental norms and runs on a zero liquid discharge (ZLD) system: all wash water is treated and reused, and none is released into the environment.

Why?

Across Karnataka, plastic waste from municipalities, industries and farms is piling up. Much of it ends up in landfills or is burnt in the open, even though it can be recycled into valuable raw material.

What's missing is a local, reliable recycler: one that buys waste consistently, meets environmental norms, and produces high-quality granules that manufacturers can use with confidence. It also has to work fairly with the informal collectors and waste pickers who gather much of this waste, not just with municipalities and industries. We started MINWAS to be that recycler for North Karnataka.

Who?

Founded by a climate scientist and a former Bosch engineer, both with years of experience in Germany, MINWAS brings German quality discipline to Indian recycling.
