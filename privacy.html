Last updated: 1 October 2026

Who we are

What we collect

When you submit an enquiry form or contact us, we collect the details you provide: your name, company or organisation, phone or WhatsApp number, email address, location, and information about the material or products you are interested in. If you contact us by WhatsApp, phone or email, we receive the details you share there.

Why we use it

We use your data only to respond to your enquiry, arrange pickups, deliveries or samples, prepare quotes, maintain records required for business and regulatory compliance, and improve our website. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing without your consent.

Who we share it with

We share your data only with service providers who help us run our business – such as website hosting, form and email services, and logistics partners for pickups or deliveries – and only as far as needed. Some of these providers may store data on servers outside India. We may also disclose data where required by law or by a government authority.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as needed to handle your enquiry and any business relationship that follows, and for as long as required by law (for example, tax and compliance records). After that, we delete it.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, update or delete your personal data, or withdraw your consent at any time, by writing to minwas.info@gmail.com. Withdrawing consent does not affect anything we did before you withdrew it, but we may no longer be able to respond to your enquiry.

Grievances

If you have a concern about how we handle your data, write to minwas.info@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your data from unauthorised access, loss or misuse.

Children

This website is intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated.
